DESIGN ARCHIVE
002 / 01 · Origins & influences

Kairin’s personal website

I’ve loved physical media since I was a kid. Books came first for me, and around 2023 I started getting more into the music side of things, including vinyl. So when I started designing my own website, I wanted browsing it to feel a little like looking through my shelf. And since it’s my shelf, of course it’s Michael Jackson hahaha.

The blue sleeve and white fedora on the homepage take their cue from the Smooth Criminal music video and the MJ poster on my wall. I picked four MJ albums as the way into my work. Dangerous opens the story behind this website, Invincible takes you to the privacy compliance analyser, and HIStory takes you to my policy writing. For CatMana, I picked Xscape.

002 / 02 · Design evolution

From a music player to a record shelf

The first version leaned into the pink and black of my imagined ’80s music player. I liked the idea of browsing releases and having Human Nature along for the ride. When I brought in the blue poster, I had a more personal starting point for the homepage. The tape, piano keys and record peeking out of the sleeve are details I’d stop to look at if I saw the cover in a shop.

ARCHIVE 002 / ORIGINAL CASE STUDY
002 / 03 · Typography evidence

Even the fonts are Michael Jackson

I wanted the first thing you see on my homepage to take its cue from what I think is the world’s most iconic album: Thriller. That’s why “Hi, I am Kairin” has that sweeping, Mac James-inspired script. The fedora beside it is my nod to Smooth Criminal.

For the lettering below the blue artwork, my design inspiration is Bad. I wanted a recruiter reading my experience or opening a case file on their phone to be able to read the whole thing comfortably. And if I was going to pick a second style anyway, why not Bad? Hahaha.

ARCHIVE 002 / ORIGINAL CASE STUDY
002 / 04 · Project navigation

Browsing the projects

I also changed what happens when you choose a record. Each album has its project title beside it, and you can tap either one to open the file. Someone looking for my policy writing can go straight there without knowing my taste in music. Someone who recognises the covers can browse exactly the way I would.

Ideally, a recruiter leaves knowing a little more about my work. If they also leave as a Michael Jackson fan, even better. 🐥
